Common questions

How does my balance work?

Your account is managed by your administrators. Your balance is always the exact sum of every entry in your transaction history — entries are permanent and are never edited or removed.

How do transfers and deposits work?

Submitting a transfer or deposit creates a request with a reference code (like TR-ABC123). An administrator is notified immediately and reviews each request individually. You can track any request's status from the Transfers or External deposits page.

Why is my request still pending?

Every request is reviewed by a person, not processed automatically, so timing depends on your administrator's availability. If a request has been pending for a while, send a message above with the reference code.

How do I reset my password?

While signed in, go to change password and update it directly — no email needed. If you're locked out, the login page has an email reset link. You'll still need your Case ID to sign in afterwards — if you've lost that, send your administrator a message above.
